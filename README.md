## The entry

> **Senior Vice President of Apologizing to Geese**

A blind judge compares titles two at a time and picks the weirder, funnier one.
This one won a pairwise tournament of five candidates: corporate gravitas
(Senior Vice President) slammed into an absurdly specific duty (apologizing
to geese — famously the least forgiving birds on earth). It reads like a real
job, it pictures instantly, and the punch lands on the last word.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes the title to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`
(default `/output/entry.txt`) and validates the 60-character gate first.

```sh
node main.ts
```
